Fluffkin Catch

Something is knocking on your walls! Cracks glow, the wall bursts open, and fluffy little Fluffkins hop into your room. Catch them with bubbles and fill your jar.

How to play
  1. Pick Play in my room or Play in a calm world.
  2. Grab the play mat (pinch it, or point and hold the trigger) and put it on a table or the floor. The buttons behind it make it bigger or smaller.
  3. Press Lock & Play. The mat now stays put. To move it again, hold the small padlock for 2 seconds.
  4. Put the jar mat on a table or the floor and press Lock & Play. Choose 2 minutes or No timer, then press Start.
  5. Listen for the knocking: cracks spread, then the wall bursts open and the Fluffkins peek out, jump down and bounce around. In Play in my room the holes open in your real walls (from the Quest room scan); without a room scan, and in VR, they use a pretend playroom.
  6. Blow bubbles at them: pull the trigger (a bubble blaster sits in your hand) or pinch your fingers with a bare hand. Bubbles curve a little towards the nearest Fluffkin.
  7. A caught Fluffkin floats into your jar. Catch the same colour again and again for a combo, up to ×5. Hold stop to finish early.
Click a Fluffkin to blow a bubble at it. Right-drag (or two fingers) to look around.
