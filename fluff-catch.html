Fluffkin Catch

Little black holes pop open around your room and cute Fluffkins pour out! Wave after wave, then the Fluffkin Rush. Aim your bubble blaster and fill your jar.

How to play
  1. Pick Play in my room or Play in a calm world.
  2. Grab the play mat (pinch it, or point and hold the trigger) and put it on a table or the floor. The buttons behind it make it bigger or smaller.
  3. Press Lock & Play. The mat now stays put. To move it again, hold the small padlock for 2 seconds.
  4. Put the jar mat on a table or the floor and press Lock & Play. On the sign, choose 2 minutes or No timer, switch the Music on or off, then press Start.
  5. Watch for space holes: little black holes tear open in the air and Fluffkins pour out, flung in all directions. They come in waves that get bigger (up to three holes at once), and the last 22 seconds are the Fluffkin Rush. Then they bounce around the floor and furniture, wave at you cheekily and do zoomies. In Play in my room they use your real floor and furniture (from the Quest room scan); without one, and in VR, they use a pretend playroom.
  6. Aim and blow bubbles at them: pull the trigger (a bubble blaster sits in your hand) or pinch your fingers with a bare hand. Tap for a small, fast bubble; hold to blow a big one (easier to hit, a bit slower), then let go. Bubbles go where you point, slow down, then float and drift. The dotted line shows where yours will go, and turns the Fluffkin's colour when you are on target.
  7. A caught Fluffkin floats into your jar. Catch the same colour again and again for a combo, up to ×5. A rare golden Fluffkin is quick and worth 500 (it keeps your combo going). Long shots and catching one in mid-air score a little extra. Holes swallow bubbles, so aim at the Fluffkins! Hold stop to finish early.
Point at a Fluffkin and click to blow a bubble; hold the button to blow a big one. Right-drag (or two fingers) to look around.
