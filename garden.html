Little Garden

A little flower bed with a watering can and a rain cloud. Water the seeds and watch them grow into flowers, then butterflies come to visit.

How to play
  1. Pick Play in my room or Play in a calm world.
  2. Grab the play mat (pinch it, or point and hold the trigger) and put it on a table or the floor. The buttons behind it make it bigger or smaller.
  3. Press Lock & Play. The mat now stays put. To move it again, hold the small padlock for 2 seconds.
  4. Pick up the watering can and tip it over a plant to water it. With a controller, hold any button to carry it.
  5. Each plant grows a little each time it gets enough water: seed, shoot, bud, then a flower.
  6. Press the cloud button to make it rain on the whole bed.
  7. When flowers bloom, butterflies come. Hold your finger still and one may land on it. Hold New seeds to start again.
