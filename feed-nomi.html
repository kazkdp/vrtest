Feed Nomi

Nomi is hungry! Give Nomi the food in the thought bubble, or any food at all.

How to play
  1. Pick Play in my room or Play in a calm world.
  2. Grab the play mat (pinch it, or point and hold the trigger) and put it on a table or the floor. The buttons behind it make it bigger or smaller.
  3. Press Lock & Play. The mat now stays put. To move it again, hold the small padlock for 2 seconds.
  4. Pick up a food and bring it to Nomi's mouth. Nomi watches it and opens wide.
  5. The thought bubble shows what Nomi would love most: that one earns extra hearts and a happy spin.
  6. Every food is welcome. After five, Nomi is full and does a little burp.
Drag the food to Nomi's mouth. Right-drag to look around.
