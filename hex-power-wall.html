How to play
  1. Pick Play in my room or Play in a calm world.
  2. Grab the play mat (pinch it, or point and hold the trigger) and put it on a table or the floor. The buttons behind it make it bigger or smaller.
  3. Press Lock & Play. The mat now stays put. To move it again, hold the small padlock for 2 seconds.
  4. Each stage has a colour (the ring around the reactor). Tap the hexagons of that colour with a finger or the controller tip.
  5. Their energy races into the reactor. Fill it to clear the stage. Hexagons with a star count double.
  6. Other colours just wobble. From stage 3 the hexagons start hopping around.
  7. There is a timer: if it runs out the stage simply starts again. Press Timer on in the corner to switch it off for relaxed play.
