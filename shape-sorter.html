Shape Sorter

A big toy table with a shape box, a puzzle board and coloured buckets. Each round sets out a new toy with more shapes.

How to play
  1. Pick Play in my room or Play in a calm world.
  2. Grab the play mat (pinch it, or point and hold the trigger) and put it on a table or the floor. The buttons behind it make it bigger or smaller.
  3. Press Lock & Play. The mat now stays put. To move it again, hold the small padlock for 2 seconds.
  4. Pinch a shape (or make a fist around it) to pick it up. With a controller, touch it with the glowing tip and hold any button.
  5. Bring it close to where it belongs and it slides in by itself: the matching hole in the box, the matching outline on the puzzle board, or the bucket of the same colour.
  6. Pieces that go to the wrong place simply float back. Rounds change between the three toys and slowly add shapes, up to 8, from 13 different shapes.
Drag each piece to where it belongs. Right-drag to look around.
