How to play
  1. Choose Play in my room. A big glowing wall appears in front of you (it starts at the size you last used on the Power Wall).
  2. Drag the yellow corners to stretch it, grab the bar on top to move it, or look at a real wall and press Wall to snap it flat.
  3. Press Lock & Play, then touch an experiment on the wall.
  4. Power Path: slide your hand over the hexes to lay a path from the gold power hex to the lamp. Touch the path to go back.
  5. Chain Reaction: touch a hex and every touching hex of the same colour bursts too. Big chains leave a star hex: touch it for a blast.
  6. Hex Match: look at the cards, then find the pairs.
  7. Hex Beats: touch hexes to light them; the sweeping light plays them. Bottom row drum, next row clap, then notes going up. Hold Clear to empty it.
  8. Hold Games in the bottom corner to choose another. Hold the padlock beside the wall to change the wall.
Click (or click and drag) on the wall to play. Hold Games to switch game. Right-drag to look around.
