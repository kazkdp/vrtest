Bubble Pop

A bubble table with a soap tub, bubble wands and a friendly bubble machine. Make bubbles, watch them float, and pop them.

How to play
  1. Pick Play in my room or Play in a calm world.
  2. Grab the play mat (pinch it, or point and hold the trigger) and put it on a table or the floor. The buttons behind it make it bigger or smaller.
  3. Press Lock & Play. The mat now stays put. To move it again, hold the small padlock for 2 seconds.
  4. Pick up a bubble wand from the tub. Wave it slowly to grow one big bubble (it lets go when you stop), or quickly for lots of little ones.
  5. When the soap runs out, dip the ring back into the tub.
  6. Press the big pink button on the bubble machine for a burst of bubbles.
  7. Touch a bubble to pop it. Swish your hand quickly and the air pushes bubbles away. Bubbles land on the mat and pop by themselves after a while.
Click bubbles to pop them. Drag a wand and move it quickly to make bubbles; drag it over the tub to add soap. Right-drag to look around.
