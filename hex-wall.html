Hex Wall

Four glowing hexagon games on one wall: Hex Power, Hex Crush, Power Path and Hex Match. Set the wall up once, lock it, then pick a game on the wall itself. Win up to 3 stars on every level.

How to play
  1. Choose Play in my room. A big glowing wall appears in front of you (it starts at the size you last used on the Power Wall).
  2. Drag the yellow corners to stretch it, grab the bar on top to move it, or look at a real wall and press Wall to snap it flat.
  3. Press Lock & Play, then touch a game on the wall.
  4. Hex Power: tap the lit hexes of the stage colour to fill the reactor.
  5. Hex Crush: slide one hex onto a neighbour (or tap one, then the other) to make a line of 3 or more of a colour. Collect the colours shown at the top.
  6. Power Path: slide your hand over the hexes to lay a rainbow path from the gold power hex to the lamp, picking up the stars. Touch the path to go back.
  7. Hex Match: look at the cards, then find the pairs. Each picture plays its own note.
  8. Music on / Music off (bottom right) turns the backing beat on or off.
  9. Hold Games in the bottom corner to choose another. Hold the padlock beside the wall to change the wall.
Click (or click and drag) on the wall to play. Hold Games to switch game. Right-drag to look around.
