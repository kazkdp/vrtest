Play Lab

Super-simple games for Meta Quest 3

Small, calm games for young children and for kids who like things predictable. Each game works the same way: set the play mat down (or the Power Wall up), lock it so it stays put, then play. No way to lose. Fluffkin Catch keeps a score and Hex Power has a gentle timer, and you can switch both timers off. Every game also shows off something different the headset can do.

1Pick a gamethen Play in my room (you still see the room) or Play in a calm world.
2Put the mat downGrab it and set it on a table or the floor (the Power Wall goes on a wall). Make it bigger or smaller.
3Lock & playThe mat stays put. Hold the small padlock for 2 seconds to move it again.

Power Wall

Like the touch walls with projectors at play centres. Put one big glowing wall on a real wall of your room: drag the yellow corners to stretch it to any size, press Wall to snap it flat, then lock it and choose a game on the wall itself. Every game fills the wall at your size. Hold Games in the corner to switch without setting up again.
