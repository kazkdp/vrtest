Play Lab

Super-simple games for Meta Quest 3

Small, calm games for young children and for kids who like things predictable. Each game works the same way: set the play mat down, lock it so it stays put, then play. No timers, no scores to beat, and no way to lose. Every game also shows off something different the headset can do.

1Pick a gamethen Play in my room (you still see the room) or Play in a calm world.
2Put the mat downGrab it and set it on a table or the floor. Make it bigger or smaller.
3Lock & playThe mat stays put. Hold the small padlock for 2 seconds to move it again.

Bubble Pop

Touch the bubbles to pop them, or pinch and wave to blow more. Every 12 pops a giant bubble brings a star.

  • Touch with any finger
  • Pinch-and-wave gesture
  • 3D sound
Play

Shape Sorter

Post each shape through its matching hole. The right hole glows, and shapes slide in by magic when close.

  • Pick up and place
  • Magnetic snapping
  • Gentle hints

Rainbow Xylophone

Tap the rainbow bars to make music, or press Play a song and follow the bouncing star.

  • Music from touch
  • Tap strength
  • Follow-along songs

Feed Nomi

Nomi is hungry! Bring food to Nomi’s mouth. The thought bubble shows Nomi’s favourite.

  • A character that reacts
  • Eyes follow the food
  • Ask and reward

Block Tower

Knock the tower down with a swipe, stack the blocks, then press Build again to watch them fly home.

  • Real physics
  • Push with your hands
  • Stack and throw

Magic Paint

Dip a finger in a paint pot, then pinch and move to paint glowing ribbons in the air.
