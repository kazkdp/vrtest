Power Wall

One big touch wall, like the projector walls at play centres. Set the wall up at the size you want, lock it, then choose from eleven games on the wall itself.

How to play
  1. Pick Play in my room or Play in a calm world.
  2. Grab the play mat (pinch it, or point and hold the trigger) and put it on a table or the floor. The buttons behind it make it bigger or smaller.
  3. Press Lock & Play. The mat now stays put. To move it again, hold the small padlock for 2 seconds.
  4. Choose Play in my room. A big glowing wall appears in front of you.
  5. Drag the yellow corners to stretch it to the size you want, and grab the bar on top to move it. Look at a real wall and press Wall to snap it flat; after that, moving it slides it along the wall.
  6. Press Lock & Play, then touch a game on the wall to start it. Every game fills the wall at the size you set.
  7. Hold Games in the bottom corner to choose another game. Hold the padlock beside the wall to change the wall again.
Click (or click and drag) on the wall to play. Hold Games to switch game. Right-drag to look around.
